01 / The big picture

Two sagas. One connected story.

The Infinity Saga builds the Avengers and ends with Thanos. The Multiverse Saga explores what happens when time branches and realities begin to overlap.

01
PHASE 01 — 2008–2012

THE HEROES AWAKEN

Tony Stark becomes Iron Man. Thor and Captain America arrive. Nick Fury brings Earth's mightiest heroes together to stop Loki's invasion of New York.

AVENGERS ASSEMBLE
PHASE 02 — 2013–2015

THE WORLD EXPANDS

Cosmic threats grow. The Infinity Stones emerge. The Guardians form. Ultron is created, Vision is born and the Avengers' actions reshape the world.

INFINITY STONES
PHASE 03 — 2016–2019

THE FALL & THE SNAP

The Avengers split. Thanos collects all six Stones and erases half of all life. Five years later, the surviving heroes reverse the Snap — at a terrible cost.

ENDGAME
PHASE 04 — 2021–2022

A WORLD AFTER THE BLIP

New heroes rise. Sam accepts the shield. Wanda's grief reshapes reality. Loki's escape leads to the TVA and the branching Multiverse.

NEW GENERATION
PHASE 05 — 2023–2025

REALITIES COLLIDE

Incursions, variants and the TVA become central. Deadpool meets Wolverine. The Thunderbolts form a new team. The Fantastic Four arrive from another universe.

THE NEXT CHAPTER — 2026

DOOMSDAY APPROACHES

02 / The complete journey

From the first Avenger to Endgame

The Infinity Saga, in chronological MCU story order (release years are shown on each entry). These are the foundations that make the later crossover matter.

02
Showing all story entries
01
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2011

Captain America: The First Avenger

During World War II, Steve Rogers volunteers for a super-soldier experiment and becomes Captain America. He fights HYDRA and Red Skull, who uses the Tesseract as a power source. Steve crashes a plane into the Arctic to stop a catastrophe and is frozen for decades.

WHY IT MATTERS

Steve becomes the moral centre of the Avengers. The Tesseract is later revealed to contain the Space Stone. HYDRA and the super-soldier programme echo through later stories.

CORE
02
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2008

Iron Man

Weapons inventor Tony Stark is kidnapped and builds an armoured suit to escape. He returns home, shuts down Stark Industries' weapons division and defeats Obadiah Stane. Tony publicly reveals that he is Iron Man.

WHY IT MATTERS

The MCU begins with Tony's transformation. Nick Fury approaches him about the Avengers Initiative. Tony's genius, technology and sacrifice later define the Infinity Saga.

CORE
03
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2008

The Incredible Hulk

Bruce Banner lives on the run while trying to control the transformation that turns him into the Hulk. Emil Blonsky becomes the Abomination. General Thaddeus Ross pursues Banner.

WHY IT MATTERS

Introduces Bruce Banner and Ross, who returns much later as a major political figure. Tony Stark's cameo connects the film to the shared universe.

OPTIONAL CONTEXT
04
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2010

Iron Man 2

Tony struggles with the effects of the arc reactor and faces rival inventor Ivan Vanko. Natasha Romanoff is introduced as a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent. Tony studies his father Howard's research and discovers a new element.

WHY IT MATTERS

Natasha becomes a core Avenger. S.H.I.E.L.D. is revealed as the organisation helping assemble the team.

USEFUL
05
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2011

Thor

Thor is banished to Earth after acting arrogantly and provoking a conflict. He learns humility and becomes worthy of Mjolnir again. Loki discovers painful truths about his origins and resents Thor's place in Asgard.

WHY IT MATTERS

Introduces Asgard, the Nine Realms and Loki — one of the most important characters in the Multiverse Saga.

CORE
06
PHASE 1 · FILM · 2012

The Avengers

Loki steals the Tesseract and opens a portal for the Chitauri to invade New York. Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye are brought together by Nick Fury. They overcome their differences and stop the invasion.

WHY IT MATTERS

The original Avengers are formed. The Battle of New York changes the world and leaves Earth aware of cosmic threats.

CORE
07
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2013

Iron Man 3

Tony struggles with trauma after the Battle of New York and confronts the apparent Mandarin and Aldrich Killian's Extremis technology.

WHY IT MATTERS

Shows that the Avengers' victory came with psychological consequences, especially for Tony.

OPTIONAL CONTEXT
08
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2013

Thor: The Dark World

Thor battles Malekith, who seeks to use the Aether. The Aether is later identified as the Reality Stone and placed with the Collector.

WHY IT MATTERS

Important Infinity Stone lore. The film also deepens Thor and Loki's complicated relationship.

STONE LORE
09
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2014

Captain America: The Winter Soldier

Steve discovers HYDRA has infiltrated S.H.I.E.L.D. His old friend Bucky Barnes is revealed to be the brainwashed Winter Soldier. Steve, Natasha and Sam Wilson expose the conspiracy.

WHY IT MATTERS

S.H.I.E.L.D. collapses. Bucky's past and Sam's bond with Steve lead into later Captain America and Thunderbolts stories.

CORE
10
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2014

Guardians of the Galaxy

Peter Quill, Gamora, Drax, Rocket and Groot form a team while pursuing an Orb containing the Power Stone. They defeat Ronan and protect Xandar.

WHY IT MATTERS

Connects Earth to the wider cosmic universe and establishes the Power Stone and the Guardians' family bond.

CORE
11
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2017

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2

The Guardians discover Peter Quill's father is Ego, a powerful being who plans to spread himself across planets. Peter rejects him; Yondu sacrifices himself to save Peter.

WHY IT MATTERS

Completes important emotional backstory for the Guardians before Infinity War.

USEFUL
12
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2015

Avengers: Age of Ultron

Tony and Bruce create an AI defence system that becomes Ultron. The Avengers stop him in Sokovia. Wanda and Pietro Maximoff enter the story, and Vision is created with the Mind Stone.

WHY IT MATTERS

Introduces Wanda, Vision and the Mind Stone. Sokovia's destruction later fuels government pressure on superheroes.

CORE
13
PHASE 2 · FILM · 2015

Ant-Man

Scott Lang becomes Ant-Man with a suit designed by Hank Pym. Scott defeats Darren Cross and enters the Quantum Realm, then returns.

WHY IT MATTERS

The Quantum Realm becomes crucial to the time-travel plan in Endgame and to Kang's story.

CORE FOR ENDGAME
14
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2016

Captain America: Civil War

After collateral damage from superhero missions, governments propose the Sokovia Accords. Tony supports oversight; Steve opposes it. Zemo reveals Bucky was responsible for Tony's parents' deaths while brainwashed. The Avengers fracture. T'Challa and Peter Parker enter the story.

WHY IT MATTERS

The Avengers are divided when Thanos arrives. Introduces Black Panther and Spider-Man to the MCU.

CORE
15
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2016

Doctor Strange

After a crash damages his hands, surgeon Stephen Strange discovers the mystic arts. He uses the Eye of Agamotto, which contains the Time Stone, to trap Dormammu in a time loop.

WHY IT MATTERS

Introduces the Sorcerer Supreme, magic and the Time Stone — all important to the later multiverse story.

CORE
16
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2018

Black Panther

T'Challa becomes king of Wakanda and faces Erik Killmonger, who challenges the nation's isolation. T'Challa defeats him and decides Wakanda should share its knowledge and resources.

WHY IT MATTERS

Wakanda's vibranium technology and leaders become essential in the fight against Thanos.

CORE
17
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2017

Thor: Ragnarok

Thor discovers Hela, his sister, has returned to claim Asgard. Asgard is destroyed, but its people survive. Thor accepts that Asgard is its people, not a place. Loki appears to reconcile with him.

WHY IT MATTERS

The surviving Asgardians become refugees. Loki still has the Tesseract, leading directly into Infinity War.

CORE
18
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2018

Avengers: Infinity War

Thanos collects all six Infinity Stones — Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time and Soul — to erase half of all life. The Avengers and Guardians try to stop him, but Thanos completes the Snap. Many heroes disappear.

WHY IT MATTERS

The Snap creates the five-year Blip. It is the defining catastrophe of the Infinity Saga.

CORE
19
PHASE 3 · FILM · 2019

Avengers: Endgame

Five years after the Snap, the surviving heroes use the Quantum Realm to travel through time and collect the Stones. The 2014 Thanos attacks the present. Tony uses the Stones to erase Thanos and his army, sacrificing himself. Natasha dies obtaining the Soul Stone. Steve returns the Stones and chooses a life with Peggy, later passing the shield to Sam.

THE NEW STARTING POINT

Tony and Natasha are dead. Steve has retired. Sam has the shield. Thor leaves with the Guardians. Bruce is Professor Hulk. The Blip is reversed, but the world has changed. This is where the post-Endgame story begins.

03 / The new era

After Endgame: the Multiverse opens

The world is recovering from the Blip while new heroes, magical threats and alternate realities emerge.

03
20
FILM · 2019

Spider-Man: Far From Home

Peter Parker struggles with Tony Stark's legacy. Mysterio manipulates him with illusions, then exposes Peter's identity to the world.

REMEMBER

Tony's absence leaves a leadership vacuum. Peter's life becomes public and unstable.

BRIDGE
21
SERIES · 2021

WandaVision

Wanda creates a reality bubble around Westview and constructs an idealised family with Vision and twins Billy and Tommy. The illusion collapses; Wanda becomes the Scarlet Witch and studies the Darkhold.

REMEMBER

Wanda's reality-altering magic, Billy and Tommy, and the Darkhold lead into Multiverse of Madness and Agatha All Along.

CORE
22
SERIES · 2021–2023

Loki — Seasons 1 & 2

The 2012 Loki who escaped with the Tesseract is arrested by the Time Variance Authority (TVA). He meets Sylvie and learns that He Who Remains controlled the Sacred Timeline to prevent a multiversal war. Sylvie kills him, branching timelines multiply, and Loki ultimately takes responsibility for holding the branches together.

REMEMBER

This is the Multiverse Saga's foundation. TVA = timeline authority; variants = alternate versions; Loki's fate = the branching timelines are held together.

TOP PRIORITY
23
SERIES · 2021

The Falcon and the Winter Soldier

Sam initially gives up Steve's shield, but ultimately accepts the responsibility and becomes Captain America. Bucky confronts his past. John Walker becomes the government-backed Captain America for a time and later operates as US Agent.

REMEMBER

Sam is the new Captain America. Bucky and Walker later join the Thunderbolts* team.

CORE
24
FILM · 2021

Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings

Shang-Chi confronts his father Wenwu and inherits the powerful Ten Rings. Wong, Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers later investigate the Rings and discover an unexplained signal.

REMEMBER

Shang-Chi is an announced Doomsday cast member. The Rings' origin remains an open mystery.

USEFUL
25
FILM · 2021

Eternals

Ancient beings sent by the Celestials discover that Earth was being prepared for the birth of a new Celestial. Some Eternals rebel to save humanity.

REMEMBER

Expands the cosmic scale of the MCU, but is not a core prerequisite for Doomsday's known premise.

OPTIONAL COSMIC LORE
26
SERIES · 2021

Hawkeye

Clint Barton meets Kate Bishop, a young archer who becomes his protégé. Yelena Belova arrives believing Clint caused Natasha's death, then learns the truth.

REMEMBER

Introduces Kate's next-generation hero path and brings Yelena into the post-Black Widow story.

USEFUL
27
FILM · 2021

Spider-Man: No Way Home

Peter asks Doctor Strange to make the world forget his identity. The spell goes wrong and draws people from other universes who know Peter Parker is Spider-Man. Peter chooses a final spell that makes everyone forget him, including MJ and Ned.

REMEMBER

Alternate-universe characters can cross into the MCU. Peter is now alone and unknown to his closest friends.

CORE
28
FILM · 2022

Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

America Chavez can open portals between universes. Wanda, corrupted by the Darkhold, hunts her power to reach alternate versions of Billy and Tommy. Strange visits alternate realities, including one with the Illuminati. Wanda destroys the Darkhold's influence and seemingly sacrifices herself.

REMEMBER

An incursion is when universes collide and can destroy one another. America is a multiverse traveller. Wanda's fate is not fully closed.

TOP PRIORITY
29
FILM · 2022

Black Panther: Wakanda Forever

After T'Challa's death, Shuri eventually becomes the new Black Panther. Namor, ruler of the underwater nation Talokan, clashes with Wakanda over vibranium and outside threats. Shuri chooses a fragile peace.

REMEMBER

Shuri is Black Panther. Namor and Talokan are powerful forces. M'Baku is positioned to lead Wakanda.

CORE TEAM CONTEXT
30
FILM · 2023

Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania

Scott's family is pulled into the Quantum Realm and encounters Kang the Conqueror, an exiled time-travelling warlord. They stop his immediate escape; post-credit scenes show a gathering of Kang variants.

REMEMBER

Kang was once positioned as the next major saga villain. Doctor Doom is now the announced central Doomsday antagonist; do not assume Kang is the film's villain.

CONTEXT
31
FILM · 2023

Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3

The Guardians save Rocket and confront the High Evolutionary, who experimented on him. Peter returns to Earth, and Rocket leads a new Guardians team.

REMEMBER

Completes the Guardians' emotional arc. Useful, but not essential to the known Doomsday setup.

OPTIONAL
32
FILM · 2024

Deadpool & Wolverine

Deadpool learns his universe is at risk and becomes entangled with the TVA. He teams up with a Wolverine variant and encounters characters from earlier Fox Marvel films. They face Cassandra Nova, whose power threatens realities.

REMEMBER

It is the most useful recent bridge to the legacy X-Men universe and demonstrates the TVA's reach beyond the main MCU.

TOP PRIORITY
33
SERIES · 2024

Agatha All Along

Agatha returns after WandaVision and assembles a coven to walk the Witches' Road. The mysterious teen is revealed as Billy Maximoff, whose soul has found a new body.

REMEMBER

Billy exists and searches for his twin Tommy. Wanda's family story continues.

MAGIC THREAD
34
FILM · 2025

Captain America: Brave New World

Sam Wilson faces a political crisis involving President Thaddeus Ross, a conspiracy and a powerful new transformation: Red Hulk. Sam chooses to save Ross rather than simply defeat him.

REMEMBER

Sam is firmly established as Captain America. Ross remains a major political figure.

USEFUL
35
FILM · 2025

Thunderbolts*

Yelena, Bucky, Red Guardian, John Walker, Ghost and Taskmaster are drawn into a covert mission that turns out to be a trap. They encounter Bob Reynolds, who becomes Sentry. His darker persona, the Void, threatens a city. The team works together to save him and is publicly associated with the name New Avengers.

REMEMBER

The New Avengers now exist as a team. Sentry is immensely powerful but vulnerable to the Void. Yelena, Bucky and Walker are key members.

TOP PRIORITY
36
FILM · 2025

The Fantastic Four: First Steps

In a retro-futuristic alternate universe, Reed Richards, Sue Storm, Johnny Storm and Ben Grimm face Galactus and his herald Silver Surfer. The team saves its world, and Reed and Sue's son Franklin is introduced.

REMEMBER

The Fantastic Four come from a distinct universe. Reed Richards is Doctor Doom's traditional rival in Marvel lore. The film's exact connection to Doomsday should be distinguished from comic-book theories.

TOP PRIORITY
37
FILM · 2026

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Peter Parker continues as Spider-Man in a world that no longer remembers him. He fights crime full-time while his old friends move on without knowing who he is. A new threat forces a change in Peter.

REMEMBER

Updates Peter's post-No Way Home status. Treat its Doomsday connection as unconfirmed unless the film explicitly establishes one.

OPTIONAL FOR MULTIVERSE
38
SERIES · 2026

VisionQuest

Continues the Vision storyline beyond WandaVision and the White Vision thread, exploring Vision's identity and place in the world.

REMEMBER

Vision's story did not simply end with his death in Infinity War. This series closes or advances that thread; it is a character-context watch rather than a confirmed Doomsday prerequisite.

THE FINAL BRIEFING

Endgame ended an era.
Doomsday brings worlds together.

Remember these five anchors: Tony's sacrifice ended the Infinity Saga; Loki holds the branching timelines; incursions threaten entire realities; the New Avengers have emerged; and the Fantastic Four and legacy X-Men bring other universes into the picture.

Doctor Doom is Victor von Doom, not a confirmed Tony Stark variant. Marvel's official synopsis confirms heroes from three distinct universes face an existential threat — the rest is for the film to reveal.
